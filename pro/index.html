Questions pros ask

Who can join VIGO Pro?

Licensed contractors, remodelers, installers, plumbers, interior designers, architects and developers. We ask for one proof of trade: a license number, EIN or business website.

Can I use promo codes on top of Pro pricing?

No. Your Pro price is already the best price, every day, so there is nothing to wait for.

I already have a VIGO business account. Do I need to apply again?

No. If your business account was approved, you are already VIGO Pro. Sign in and you will see 10% off the site price on every product.

Can you ship to the job site?

Yes, and delivery is free. Add the site as the shipping address. Delivery usually takes 5–8 days. Picking a delivery date is coming soon.

Do you offer tax-exempt purchasing?

Yes. Upload your resale certificate in your account.

What if my client changes their mind?

Uninstalled items in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery; a 15% restocking fee applies. Report shipping damage within 48 hours and the Pro desk will arrange a replacement.
