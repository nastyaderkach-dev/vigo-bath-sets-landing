Questions

How do the savings work?

Add any pieces from the Matte Black Collection. Your highest-priced piece is full price. The next highest-priced piece is 3% off, the next 7% off, and the fourth and every piece after that 10% off. Each unit counts as a piece, so two faucets are two pieces. Accessories under $50, like drains and soap dispensers, count as pieces but aren’t discounted.

Do bathroom and kitchen pieces count together?

Yes. Any matte black piece in the collection counts, whichever room it’s for.

Can I use a promo code too?

Collection savings don’t combine with promo codes. At checkout you get whichever is better for your order. [Rule to confirm with VIGO.]

What if I return a piece?

The savings are recalculated for the pieces you keep, and the refund is adjusted. Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 14 days; a 15% restocking fee applies. [Return rule to confirm with VIGO.]

How is my order delivered?

Free delivery in the continental U.S., shower doors and panels included. Most orders ship within 1–3 business days.
